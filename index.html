How to Register for Clubs

Registration for student activities and clubs is managed securely through Infinite Campus or Schoology group access codes.

  1. Log into your Infinite Campus Portal student or parent account.
  2. Navigate to the "School Store" or "Activities" section in the main menu.
  3. Find your club and complete registration, or join the club's Schoology Group using the provided group code on the club card!

For questions or assistance with registration, please visit the Student Activities office (Room 151) during school hours.
