How to Register for Clubs

Registration for student activities and clubs is completed through Infinite Campus.

  1. Log into your Infinite Campus Portal student account.
  2. Navigate to "Activity Registration."
  3. Find your club and click "Add to Cart."
  4. Complete the required forms.
  5. Click "Submit" to finish your registration.

For questions or assistance with registration, please visit the Student Activities office (Room 151) during school hours.
