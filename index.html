Infinite Campus Activity Registration

Step-by-step guide to register for Conant High School clubs & activities

Registration is Mandatory!

Students must complete Infinite Campus registration & signed waivers after initial club meetings to participate.

Open Infinite Campus

Registration Steps

1

Log In & Navigate to Activity Registration

Log into your Infinite Campus Student Portal (or Parent Portal). Open the side menu and go to:

Menu Products Activity Student Reg

Parents can also select Activity Parent Reg or go through School Store ➔ Activity Registration.

2

Select Your Club or Activity

Browse the list of available Conant High School clubs & intramurals (e.g. Ping Pong Club, DECA, Coding Club, Ultimate Frisbee). Click the "View" button next to the activity you want to join.

3

Complete Required District 211 Waivers

Select the Recipient (Student Name) and review/acknowledge the mandatory forms:

  • Concussion Care Information & Acknowledgment (IHSA / District Policy)
  • Academic Eligibility Policy (Must pass 5 academic courses weekly & maintain 2.0 GPA)
  • District 211 Extracurricular Code of Conduct (Respectful behavior & substance rules)
4

Add to Cart & Submit Registration

Click "Add to Cart" (Total Price: $0.00 for standard clubs). Navigate to your Cart/Checkout and click "Submit" to complete your official registration.

Need Help Registering? Visit the Conant Student Activities Office near the Main Entrance, contact your club sponsor directly, or email the Student Activities Director.
